Company History

Building Brighter Futures Since

2021
(Previously AsquareB Educational Solutions)

AsquareB Knowledge Orbit Pvt. Ltd. was established with a vision to transform education by combining academic excellence, career intelligence, and technology-driven learning into one integrated ecosystem. Founded by passionate education professionals, the organization was created to bridge the gap between traditional education and the skills required for success in the 21st century.

Over the years, AsquareB has evolved from a tuition and academic support centre into a comprehensive EdTech and Education Services Company, offering academic coaching, career counselling, educational consultancy, entrance examination preparation, technology training, student development programs, and AI-powered educational services. With multiple learning centres across Bengaluru and a growing digital presence, AsquareB continues to expand its impact while remaining committed to quality education, innovation, and student success.

Where We Began
Past Achievements

Building Strong Foundations

Since its inception, AsquareB has achieved several significant milestones:

  • Successfully guided hundreds of students toward academic excellence and career success.
  • Established multiple educational centres across Bengaluru.
  • Built a reputation for personalized learning and concept-based teaching.
  • Introduced structured Career Counselling and Educational Consultancy services.
  • Developed specialized coaching programs for School, PUC, Competitive Exams, and Technology Education.
  • Integrated digital teaching methodologies and smart classroom practices.
  • Expanded into technology-driven education through AI-enabled student support and digital learning initiatives.
  • Earned the trust of students, parents, educational institutions, and community organizations through transparency, quality, and ethical educational practices.
Additional Achievement
  • Maintained an outstanding academic track record with no student failures in School (SSLC/CBSE/ICSE) and PUC Board Examinations among AsquareB students to date, reflecting our commitment to quality teaching, continuous assessment, personalized mentoring, and student success.
  • Achieved consistently high pass rates through concept-based learning, regular mock examinations, doubt-clearing sessions, and individualized academic support.
  • Successfully guided students to perform confidently in board examinations while fostering strong academic foundations and lifelong learning skills.

Where We're Headed
Future Vision

Empowering Future-Ready Learners

Our vision is to become one of India's leading EdTech-enabled Education Companies, delivering innovative, technology-driven, and student-centric learning experiences that empower learners to excel academically, professionally, and personally.

We Aspire To:
  • Establish a nationwide network of learning centres.
  • Build one of India's most trusted AI-powered education platforms.
  • Develop intelligent learning systems using Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Learning Analytics.
  • Launch a comprehensive Learning Management System (LMS) and mobile learning application.
  • Create AI-powered Career Intelligence Reports and personalized learning pathways.
  • Develop multilingual AI assistants for students, parents, teachers, and educational institutions.
  • Introduce adaptive learning technologies that personalize education for every learner.
  • Expand our offerings in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Robotics, Coding, Cyber Security, Entrepreneurship, and Emerging Technologies.
  • Partner with schools, colleges, universities, industries, and global education organizations to deliver world-class educational services.
  • Build an integrated digital ecosystem connecting students, parents, educators, counsellors, and institutions through smart technology.

Our mission is to create future-ready learners who are academically strong, technologically skilled, ethically grounded, and prepared to thrive in a rapidly evolving world.

Our Core Values

Principles that anchor our culture, direct our choices, and shape our community.

01

Student First

Every decision we make begins with one question: Will this help our students learn, grow, and succeed? We are committed to placing learners at the heart of everything we do.

02

Academic Excellence

We strive to deliver high-quality education through concept-based learning, experienced educators, personalized mentoring, and continuous academic improvement.

03

Innovation & EdTech

We embrace innovation by integrating Artificial Intelligence, digital learning platforms, learning analytics, and emerging technologies to create engaging, personalized, and future-ready educational experiences.

04

Integrity & Transparency

We uphold the highest standards of honesty, ethics, fairness, and accountability in our academic, professional, and business practices.

05

Lifelong Learning

We foster curiosity, critical thinking, creativity, and continuous self-improvement, encouraging learners and educators alike to embrace lifelong learning.

06

Personalized Learning

We recognize that every learner is unique. Our educational approach focuses on individual strengths, learning styles, career aspirations, and personal development.

07

Career Readiness

We prepare students not only for examinations but also for higher education, professional careers, entrepreneurship, and lifelong success through career guidance and skill development.

08

Technology with Purpose

We leverage technology responsibly to enhance teaching, learning, assessment, communication, and student support while keeping human mentorship at the center.

09

Collaboration & Inclusivity

We believe meaningful learning happens through collaboration among students, parents, educators, institutions, industry partners, and communities. We respect diversity and promote equal opportunities for all.

10

Continuous Improvement

We continuously evaluate, innovate, and improve our programs, teaching methodologies, technology platforms, and student services to meet evolving educational needs.

11

Social Responsibility

We believe education is a powerful tool for positive social change. Through community engagement, scholarship initiatives, and educational outreach, we strive to make quality education more accessible and impactful.

12

Excellence Through Results

Our commitment to student success is reflected in our personalized mentoring, continuous assessments, and our strong academic record, including maintaining no student failures among our enrolled School and PUC Board Examination students to date.

13

Leadership & Empowerment

We inspire students to become confident leaders, ethical professionals, innovative thinkers, and responsible global citizens capable of making meaningful contributions to society.

14

Future-Focused Mindset

We prepare learners for a rapidly changing world by equipping them with future-ready competencies in Artificial Intelligence, Digital Technologies, Communication, Leadership, Entrepreneurship, and Problem-Solving.
