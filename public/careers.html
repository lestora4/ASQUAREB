Life at AsquareB

AsquareB Knowledge Orbit Private Limited is an integrated education, career counselling, admissions and skill-development organisation. We provide services to school students, college students, graduates, parents and working professionals through specialised programs in career counselling, academic education, global admissions, competitive examinations, technology training and professional development.

Our objective is to build a professional team capable of managing our growing operations and delivering quality services.

At AsquareB, you are trusted with real responsibilities, encouraged to share your ideas and given opportunities to make a meaningful contribution. Your work matters, your contributions are valued, and every role plays an important part in creating better experiences and opportunities for those we serve.
