Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about ASQUAREB's programs, fees, learning approach, and student support.
What is ASQUAREB Knowledge Orbit?
ASQUAREB Knowledge Orbit Private Limited is a comprehensive education and career development organization offering academic coaching, career counselling, admission guidance, entrance coaching, online education, vocational training, technology courses, and student development programs—all under one roof.
  • Tagline: "Empowering Learners. Shaping Careers. Transforming Futures."
Why should I choose ASQUAREB?
We focus on holistic student development by combining quality education, personalized mentoring, career counselling, skill development, and technology-enabled learning to help students succeed academically and professionally.

Our strengths include:
  • Experienced and qualified faculty
  • Personalized attention for every student
  • Small batch sizes wherever possible
  • Career counselling by certified professionals
  • Regular assessments and progress tracking
  • Parent engagement and performance updates
  • Online, offline, hybrid, and home tuition options
  • Technology-enabled learning environments
What is your vision and mission?
Vision: To become India's most trusted educational ecosystem by empowering learners with knowledge, skills, values, and career opportunities.

Mission:

• Deliver quality education that is accessible to every learner

• Bridge the gap between academics and employability

• Provide scientific career guidance and counselling

• Promote lifelong learning and professional development

• Prepare students for the evolving demands of the future
Who can join ASQUAREB?
Our programs are designed for learners at every stage, including:
  • School Students (Class 1–10)
  • PUC Students
  • Undergraduate Students
  • Postgraduate Students
  • Working Professionals
  • Career Changers
  • Parents seeking educational guidance
  • Students returning to education after a gap
Whether you're aiming to improve academic performance, prepare for competitive exams, develop new skills, or plan your career, we have programs tailored to your needs.
How can I take admission at ASQUAREB?
Taking admission is simple:

Step 1: Contact our Admission Team through website, WhatsApp, phone, or walk-in visit.

Step 2: Attend a FREE Counselling Session.

Step 3: Based on the counselling, we'll recommend the most suitable course, batch, and learning mode.

Step 4: Submit the required documents.

Step 5: Complete the fee payment.

Step 6: Receive your admission confirmation, student ID, and batch details.
Can I visit the centre before admission?
Absolutely! We encourage students and parents to visit our centres before taking admission. During your visit, you can:
  • Meet our counsellors
  • Interact with faculty
  • Explore classrooms
  • Understand our teaching methodology
  • Clarify fee details
  • Discuss career goals
Appointments are recommended for a smoother experience.
Is counselling compulsory before admission?
For most programs, we strongly recommend attending a counselling session before admission. This helps us:
  • Understand the student's academic level
  • Identify learning gaps
  • Recommend the right course
  • Suggest suitable batches
  • Guide students toward appropriate career pathways
Our counselling sessions are designed to help students make informed decisions rather than simply selecting a course.
What documents are required for admission?
Generally, students may be required to submit:
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Previous marks card
  • School/College ID (if applicable)
  • Transfer Certificate (where required)
  • Address Proof
  • Parent/Guardian ID (for school students)
Additional documents may be requested depending on the course.

What is Career Counselling?
Career Counselling is a structured process that helps students understand their interests, abilities, personality, values, and career aspirations to make informed academic and professional decisions.

At ASQUAREB, our counselling sessions help students:
  • Discover their strengths
  • Explore suitable career options
  • Select the right academic pathway
  • Understand future job opportunities
  • Build a long-term career plan

  • SSLC/Class 10 students choosing streams

  • Undergraduate students planning higher studies
  • Graduates seeking career direction
  • Working professionals considering career changes
  • Parents who want expert guidance for their children

What is Online Education?
Online Education is a flexible mode of learning where students can pursue academic qualifications without attending regular classroom sessions every day. Students receive learning resources, online support, recorded lectures (where available), academic guidance, and appear for examinations as per the university guidelines.

Online Education is suitable for learners who require flexibility due to work, family responsibilities, or other commitments.
What is NIOS?
The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) is an open schooling system that offers flexible education opportunities for learners who cannot pursue regular schooling or who prefer an alternative mode of education.

NIOS provides Secondary (equivalent to Class 10) and Senior Secondary (equivalent to Class 12) programs, along with vocational and other educational opportunities. NIOS is suitable for school dropouts, working students, sports persons, artists, and learners requiring flexible education.

At ASQUAREB, we focus on more than just improving marks. Our approach combines concept-based teaching, personalized mentoring, regular assessments, career guidance, and continuous parent communication to help students achieve their academic and personal goals.

My child is weak in Mathematics. Can you help?
Yes. We first identify the student's learning gaps through observation and assessments. Based on the findings, we provide:

  • Progress monitoring
The goal is to improve both understanding and confidence. Our Academic Recovery Programs are specifically designed for students who need extra support.
